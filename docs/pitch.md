# Pitch

## One-liner

A home base for every connected device on your network: it finds them and gets you to each one in a click, so you never have to remember an IP or hunt for a login page again.

## Elevator pitch

Your network is full of little web servers — your TV, cameras, speakers, router, NAS, gaming console, computers — each with its own local UI buried behind an IP address you can never remember. Petaurum scans your network, identifies what's there, and gives you one clean dashboard that links straight to every device.

It starts as a launchpad: find a device, click, you're in. Over time it learns each device's API, stores your credentials securely, and lets you actually talk to them, all from one place, running locally on your own network.

## Principles

- **Edge first.** Runs on your network. Nothing has to leave it.
- **Native per platform.** Each app is built for its platform, and they share specs rather than code.
- **Works with nothing configured.** The first launch should already show something useful.

## Inspirations

OvrC, Home Assistant.
